1 Executive Summary & Problem Landscape

Executive Synopsis: Modern university students actively register for hackathons, paper symposiums, case challenges, research workshops, and cultural sprints. However, their registrations, milestone deadlines, venues, and schedules are fragmented across 6+ portals (Devpost, Unstop, Luma, Devfolio, EasyChair, Google Forms, and WhatsApp groups). MyCampus unifies this chaotic journey into an intelligent, zero-lag, high-aesthetic command center.

The Fragmentation Landscape: Four Critical Failure Points

Failure Point Observed Student Reality MyCampus Engineering Solution
1. Scattered Portals Students track hackathons on Devpost, paper submissions on EasyChair, workshops on Luma, and case rounds on Unstop. Bookmarks and IDs get lost. Unified Journey Timeline: Consolidated multi-platform dashboard pulling all registrations into single source of truth.
2. Multi-Stage Deadline Loss Competitions have 4–6 rounds (Abstract, Mentorship, PPT submission, Pitch). Students miss Round 2 submissions while tracking only the kickoff date. Multi-Phase Milestone Architecture: Linear progress steppers, sub-deadlines, audio checkmarks, and 48-hour Urgency Radar.
3. Venue & Schedule Clashes Overlapping presentation slots across physical campus blocks (e.g. R&D Block to Old Academic Block) with zero transit buffer. Intelligent Clash Radar: Proximity algorithms detect <6h conflicts and physical building walking friction with AI mitigation advice.
4. Day-of-Event Disarray Last-minute panic for Wi-Fi credentials, registration barcodes, emergency room desks, presentation dongles, and team passes. Survival Mode Live HUD: Focus timer, dynamic SVG QR boarding pass, Wi-Fi 1-click copy, emergency contacts, and pitch checklist.

High-Level Architecture & Tech Stack

MyCampus is engineered with a strict Zero-Build, Pure Native ES6+ Module architecture. It runs natively in any modern browser without Webpack, Vite, Babel, or complex bundling overhead. This guarantees 100% instant load performance, zero compilation lags, and robust offline-first functionality.

Frontend & Aesthetic Foundation
  • Styling: Tailwind CSS + Custom Obsidian Midnight tokens
  • Typography: Playfair Display, Plus Jakarta Sans, JetBrains Mono
  • Aesthetics: Glassmorphism (backdrop-filter: blur(16px)), mesh gradients, smooth micro-interactions
  • Sound Synthesizer: Zero-dependency Web Audio API procedural audio
State, Persistence & Standards
  • State Management: Centralized Event Bus pattern in src/app.js
  • Local Storage Engine: Resilient LocalStorage schema with backup/restore
  • Calendar Standard: RFC 5545 .ics exporter for Google, Apple, Outlook
  • AI NLP Engine: Regex & rule-based contextual entity extraction

2 Complete Feature Catalog & Specifications (Features 1 to 3)

Feature 1: Multi-Phase Milestone Journey Architecture
Flagship Core

Traditional calendar applications commit the single-timestamp fallacy—representing a multi-week competition as an isolated point in time. In reality, modern hackathons and symposiums are progressive multi-phase journeys.

  • Progressive Milestone Steppers: Every event visualizes its full lifecycle through horizontal interactive steppers: Registration ➔ Proposal / Abstract Due ➔ Shortlist Announcement ➔ Mentorship Review ➔ Grand Finale Pitch.
  • Audio-Tactile Deliverable Checkboxes: Checking off a milestone recalculates completion percentage in real time, increments student XP, triggers a harmonic Web Audio chime, and dynamically advances the event stage.
  • 48-Hour Urgency Radar: Live ticking badges spotlight imminent deliverables due in under 48 hours, styled in amber/red alert beacons.
  • Auto-Completion Transition: When 100% of milestones are checked off, the event automatically transitions to the completed stage.
Feature 2: The Quad-View Collegiate Command Center
4 Dynamic Views

Four synchronized perspectives designed to give students complete control over their academic commitments:

2.1 Journey Timeline View (Flagship)

Grouped chronological flows with status badges, squad avatars with role badges, live venue waypoints, direct portal links, and expandable milestone cards.

2.2 Monthly Academic Calendar Grid

A full responsive monthly calendar matrix with date-matched event pills colored by category. Clicking any date triggers a slide-over drawer detailing all milestones and deliverables scheduled for that day.

2.3 Lifecycle Pipeline (Kanban Board)

Five stage swimlanes: Registered ➔ In Prep ➔ Finalist ➔ Completed ➔ Podium / Won 🏆. Events advance with 1-click progression buttons. Moving to Podium / Won triggers celebratory canvas confetti and a synthesized brass victory fanfare.

2.4 Conflict & Overlap Radar

An intelligent dual-vector algorithm that scans all upcoming milestones:
• Temporal Conflict (< 6h): Flags tight deadlines where divided focus threatens submission quality (Critical < 2h, High < 4h, Medium < 6h).
• Campus Transit Bottlenecks: Computes walking time between assigned venues and flags transfers with insufficient buffer (< 15 mins).
• AI Tactical Advice: Generates automated resolution strategies (e.g. "Delegate abstract review to teammate Diya Patel or submit 24 hours in advance").

Feature 3: AI Smart Ingestion & NLP Email Parser
Automated Ingestion

Eliminates manual form entry by allowing students to paste raw, unformatted text from registration emails, WhatsApp circulars, or web portal notices.

  • Contextual Entity Extraction: Extracts Event Name, Category, Platform, Registration Code, Team Name, Venue Building, Room number, and multiple milestone dates with times.
  • One-Click Presets: Pre-loaded with 4 realistic templates (Devpost Hackathon, Unstop National AI Challenge, Luma GenAI Workshop, WhatsApp Club Circular).
  • Full JSON Backup & Restore: Provides complete data portability; users can export or import their full personal schedule and event database with one click.

A distraction-free, high-focus dashboard designed specifically for the intense hours of in-person hackathons, case presentations, and finals:

An architectural vector SVG blueprint of the IIIT Delhi campus providing geographic context to event venues:

Career & Portfolio

Converts student hackathon and symposium participation into a verified, career-ready academic brag sheet:

Feature 7: Universal Calendar Sync (.ics Export)
Interoperability

Full RFC 5545 iCalendar compliance allowing 1-click sync of all milestone deadlines to Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Microsoft Outlook. Includes automated reminder alarms set at 24 hours and 2 hours prior to every deliverable.

  • Transfers event title, description, milestone location, and squad tags into calendar invite notes.
  • Compatible with desktop, iOS, Android, and web calendar subscribers.
Feature 8: Campus Opportunity Discovery Hub & 1-Click Import
Curated Catalog

Curated repository of upcoming technical and cultural opportunities across technical institutes:

  • Categorized by Hackathon, Case Challenge, Technical Workshop, Research Conference, and Cultural Fest.
  • Displays cash prize pools (e.g. ₹5,00,000 SIH, ₹2,50,000 HackIIITD), eligibility criteria, and deadlines.
  • 1-Click Timeline Ingestion: Ingests selected opportunity into the student's personal timeline with pre-configured milestones and venue data.
Feature 9: Power-User Productivity, Command Palette (⌘K) & Shortcuts
Ergonomics

High-efficiency desktop workflows designed for collegiate power users:

  • Global Command Palette (⌘K / Ctrl+K): Instant fuzzy-search omnibar to find events, milestones, venues, or trigger modals without touching the mouse.
  • Numeric Hotkeys: Press 1 for Timeline, 2 for Calendar, 3 for Kanban, and 4 for Clash Radar.
  • Live IST Clock: Real-time Indian Standard Time clock mounted in the navigation bar to keep time-critical submissions synchronized.
Feature 10: Event Creator & Dynamic Multi-Milestone Builder
Dynamic Customization

A comprehensive event creation modal enabling students to author custom event journeys:

  • Multi-Milestone Builder: Add, remove, and configure dynamic milestone rows with Phase selection (Registration, Submission, Shortlist, Mentorship, Finale), deadline timestamps, and location details.
  • Squad Management: Define Team Name, assign team members, tag their technical roles, and assign avatar badges.
  • Campus Venue Selector: Select from pre-mapped IIITD campus buildings or designate virtual tracks.
  • Gamification Parameters: Allocate custom XP rewards (100–500 XP) and tag multi-disciplinary skills.

4 Design System, Sound Engine & Deployment

Obsidian Midnight Palette

Designed to mirror high-performance developer tools like Linear and Apple developer hubs:

  • #07090e: Deep obsidian midnight canvas
  • #0f172a: Elevated card slate surface
  • #1e293b: Sub-panel and border separator
  • #3b82f6: Primary tech blue electric accent
  • #8b5cf6: AI & intelligence purple accent
  • #10b981: Success and podium emerald beacon
Procedural Web Audio Synthesizer

Zero audio asset files (0 KB network overhead). All sounds are synthesized on the fly via the browser's AudioContext:

  • Click Feedback: Short, dampened 800Hz pulse for tactical button presses.
  • Milestone Chime: Two-tone harmonic arpeggio (523Hz ➔ 659Hz) on checkbox completion.
  • Victory Fanfare: Multi-oscillator brass chord with decay on advancing to Podium / Won.
